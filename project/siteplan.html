Site Name

VB

Vee Bites

Proposed domain: vee-bites.com

The name Vee Bites was chosen because it is short, personal, and immediately relevant to food. "Vee" represents the author's personal identity and voice, making the blog feel warm and approachable. "Bites" is a playful food reference that also hints at bite-sized, digestible content — quick recipes and honest reviews that don't waste the reader's time.

The name is easy to remember, easy to spell, and works well visually as a brand across social media and a website.

Site Purpose

Vee Bites is a personal food blog that serves two main purposes: sharing original recipes and publishing honest restaurant reviews. The site is aimed at food lovers who enjoy cooking at home as well as dining out.

Visitors can browse a collection of recipes organized by category (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, and desserts), read detailed restaurant reviews, and submit restaurants they would like Vee to review through a contact form. The site will also allow visitors to save their favourite recipes for quick future access.

Scenarios

The following are questions a typical Vee Bites visitor might ask when they arrive on the site:

  • What is a quick and delicious recipe I can make for dinner tonight?
  • Has Vee reviewed any good African or Nigerian restaurants near me?
  • How do I save a recipe so I can come back to it later?
  • Can I recommend a restaurant for Vee to visit and review?

Color Schema

The palette is warm, bold, and appetizing inspired by rich spices, toasted bread, and vibrant market produce. Each color has a specific role across the site.

Deep Red #C0392B Headings, header background, section accents
Warm Orange #E67E22 Buttons, links, hover states, highlights
Cream White #FDF6EC Page background, light sections
Charcoal #2C2C2C Body text, footer background, nav bar

Typography

Two Google Fonts are used to create a contrast between editorial personality and clean readability.

Font 1 — Headings & Display
Playfair Display
"Bold Flavours, Honest Reviews."

Used for: page titles, section headings, recipe names, blog post titles.
Weight: 700 and 900 — gives a strong editorial, magazine-style feel.

Font 2 — Body & UI
DM Sans
A clean, modern sans-serif that keeps recipe steps and review paragraphs easy to read at any screen size.

Used for: body paragraphs, navigation links, buttons, form labels, captions.
Weight: 400 (regular) and 500 (medium for labels and buttons).
